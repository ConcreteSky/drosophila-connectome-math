04 • Spiking Neuron Model

Batched Leaky Integrate-and-Fire with Dale's Law

Each simulation step (Δt = 1.0 ms, T = 60 ms) updates all N neurons in vectorized (B, N) batches on CUDA or CPU:

01

Synaptic Current

Recurrent spikes propagate via sparse CSR matmul with τsyn = 5.0 ms exponential decay: Isyn(t) = βIsyn(t-1) + WTs(t-1) + Iext.

02

Membrane Leak

Voltage integrates toward Vthresh = -45.0 mV from Vrest = -52.0 mV with membrane time constant τm = 20.0 ms.

03

Refractory Clamping

Firing neurons emit s(t) = 1, reset to Vreset = -52.0 mV, and enter a 2 ms refractory period; lesioned neurons remain clamped at rest.

04

Dale's Law Bounds

Cholinergic synapses remain clamped to [0, wmax] and GABA/glutamatergic synapses to [-wmax, 0] after every update.

09 • Plasticity & Ablation Layer

Three-Factor Eligibility Traces and Lesion Controls

Every training step and interactive feedback click routes through four transparent mechanisms:

01

Synaptic Eligibility Traces

Tracks decaying pre/post spike coincidences eij(t+1) = λeeij(t) + A+xisj − A−siyj (λe = 0.95) across Mushroom Body and Motor targets.

02

Dopamine Modulation & Baseline

Global reward (+1) or punishment (-1) is centered by moving baseline r̄ and applied as ΔW = η(r − r̄)eij(T) with cumulative weight-shift tracking.

03

Targeted Region Lesions

Silences Mushroom Body, Central Complex, or top 5% out-degree hub neurons via an active binary mask clamped at Vrest.

04

Post-Lesion Recovery Loop

Measures immediate accuracy collapse after ablation and tests whether continued reward/punishment training restores task performance.

10 • Lesion & Recovery Results

Functional Collapse and Re-Adaptation Under Brain Damage

× Immediate Ablation Deficit

Central Complex Lesion: Immediate held-out accuracy on delayed_xor dropped to 0.0% (951.0 spikes/trial) when midline routing was silenced.
Mushroom Body Lesion (Live): Disabling the memory center on 2 + 7 caused the trained network to mispredict 12 instead of 9.
Top 5% Hub Lesion: Silencing highest-degree hubs eliminated recurrent whole-brain cascades (0.0 mean spikes).

✓ Post-Lesion Plasticity Recovery

Central Complex Recovery: Continued R-STDP training restored held-out accuracy to 100.0% (938.0 spikes/trial) via alternative pathways.
Interactive Behavioral Shift: Applying Punish (-1) updates weights in real time (e.g., ΔW = 1.4491) and alters subsequent predictions.
Empirical Conclusion: Redundant anatomical pathways allow partial compensation when readout plasticity remains intact.

12 • Limitations & Conclusion

Distinguishing Biological Memorization from Algorithmic Arithmetic

The LIF model omits dendritic compartments, neuromodulator kinetics, and multi-layer surrogate backpropagation—so it tests what a fixed biological connectome can learn through a single plastic readout, not general-purpose arithmetic.

By pairing the 138,639-neuron FlyWire v783 connectome (12.1× GPU speedup at 330 MB memory) with degree-preserving controls, lesion recovery, and an honest 0–100 generalization benchmark, this project provides a transparent blueprint for biological computing research.
